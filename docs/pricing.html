Frequently Asked Questions

💳 How do I receive my License Key after purchase?
Upon completing checkout via Lemon Squeezy, a cryptographically signed Ed25519 license token is generated and delivered instantly to your email. You can activate it by setting the LICENSE_KEY environment variable.
🇹🇭 Do you support PromptPay and Thai Tax Invoices?
Yes! Lemon Squeezy supports PromptPay, credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. For formal Thai corporate Purchase Orders (PO), W-8BEN, or withholding tax certificates (ภ.ง.ด. 53/54), please contact thabot47@gmail.com.
🔒 Does Bangplanix require an internet connection to validate licenses?
No. Bangplanix validates licenses entirely offline using asymmetric cryptographic signatures (Ed25519 and NIST ML-DSA-65). It never phones home, ensuring 100% air-gapped security for sensitive banking and defense environments.
