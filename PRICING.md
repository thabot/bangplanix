## 🔑 License Activation & Cryptographic Validation

- All commercial licenses are cryptographically signed with **Ed25519** and **Post-Quantum ML-DSA-65** algorithms.
- **Air-Gapped Operation:** Bangplanix never phones home. The license token contains all signed entitlement claims and is validated locally in sub-millisecond time.
- **Environment Variable Activation:**
  ```bash
  export LICENSE_KEY="eyJsaWNlbnNlSWQiOiJMSUMtMTAwMSIs...<payload>.<signature>"
  ```
